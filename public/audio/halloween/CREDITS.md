Original game dialogue synthesized locally with FFmpeg's Flite voices:
- Stranger: `rms`, filtered and reverberated.
- Radio and help: `slt`. The same original help recording is deliberately played twice for the Monochrome II voice-imitation reveal. The mouth envelope follows that recording.

No actor recording was scraped or cloned. Elevator/CRT effects are generated by `src/halloween-audio.js`; radio clicks, footsteps, breathing and torch impact by `src/teaser-audio.js`. The short chase reuses the game's existing credited Monochrome sound. This teaser contains actual game models and an authored 3D facility; it contains no stock video.
