# Subtle Monochrome II creature introductions

The user rejected voice imitation and requested very subtle introductions to additional monsters. The three supplied Sketchfab references could not be downloaded through their official endpoints: two disable downloads, one requires authentication. The user then explicitly asked us to find similarly creepy downloadable models and use them.

Use three distinct, legally downloadable CC0 assets: Sonask and Crawlmaster from Teh_Bucket's Nightmare Monsters pack on OpenGameArt, and the hunched skull-faced Scary Quadruped Horror Creature from Summer Engine. Keep their names as internal asset identifiers. Preserve the established 25-second monitor/replay duration and 43-second shared B2 encounter.

The edit leaves the service elevator, briefly shows the long-necked creature peeking over stacked cover at 6.1–7.7 seconds, shows a distant crawler crossing at 11.65–12.35 seconds, and lets part of the skull-faced creature appear in a doorway at 16.35–18.05 seconds. Monochrome's familiar distant silhouette appears at 20–20.65 seconds, then a short black cut leads to a clean MONOCHROME II title. Each new creature appears separately for less than two seconds, mostly hidden by actual walls, cover, darkness or distance. No explanatory creature names, new powers or gameplay abilities are asserted.

Remove the copied-help recording, speaking-mouth reveal and DON'T TRUST THE VOICES tagline. Replace the stranger's framing lines and add quiet scrapes, scuttling, breath and metal sounds, scheduled on the audio clock. Keep mute, volume, reduced motion, deterministic animation, replay, load-error handling and disposal. Normalize imported bone names, correct the idle/crawl poses, convert FBX to embedded glTF, resize textures, desaturate the hide, and remove the downloaded quadruped's separate scenery.
